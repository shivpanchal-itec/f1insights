Beginner’s Guide to F1

Everything you need to know to start watching Formula 1

What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 is the highest level of international single-seat auto racing. Drivers compete in fast, advanced cars on tracks around the world, earning points for themselves and their teams throughout the season. Each race weekend includes practice sessions, qualifying, and the main Grand Prix race.

Race Weekend

A Formula 1 race weekend usually begins with practice sessions, where teams test the car and prepare for the track. Next is qualifying, which decides the order drivers start the race. The main Grand Prix takes place on Sunday, and drivers race to finish first while managing speed, tires, and pit stops.

Points system

In Formula 1, the top 10 drivers in each race earn points based on their finishing position. The winner earns 25 points, second place earns 18, and third place earns 15. Drivers and teams collect these points throughout the season. The driver with the most points becomes the Drivers’ Champion, while the team with the most combined points becomes the Constructors’ Champion.
